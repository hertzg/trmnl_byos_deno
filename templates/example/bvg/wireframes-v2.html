BVG journey board — wireframes v2 (user-story decisions)

Iteration 2: each section visualizes a follow-up question or proposed user story. Where I'm asking you to pick between options, frames render side-by-side. Section 9 & 10 are abstract timeline diagrams; the rest are device frames at native 1872 × 1404 scaled to 50 % for preview.

9 · Window concept — 1 h centered on arrive-by

Abstract timeline. The deadline T sits in the middle of the window. Candidates arriving before T (filled bars) and after T (gray bars) both appear. Candidates outside ±30 min (dashed) are dropped. Compare to the old "before-only" interpretation on the right.

NEW — centered window (this design)

arrive-by 09:30 · window 1 h
on-time arrival (in window)
arrives late but still inside window
outside window — dropped

OLD — before-only window (earlier proposal)

arrive-by 09:30 · showFromMin 60
on-time (kept)
arriving late OR too early — dropped

loses the "I'd rather arrive 5 min late than miss the train I want" planning case.

10 · Auto-hide via window geometry

As "now" advances rightward through the window, the candidate set on each side of "now" shrinks naturally. Once "now" passes T + 30, the window contains no future leave-by, the preference produces zero rows, and the visual disappears without a separate cutoff rule.

08:35 — early, full window

5 rows visible

09:25 — past T, fading

1 row visible

10:02 — past T + 30, gone

preference auto-hides; falls back to next anchor
