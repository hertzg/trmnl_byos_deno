BVG journey board — wireframes v3

Iteration 3, refining decisions from v2. Window is now asymmetric (long lead-time, short late-tail). Realtime delays and disruption remarks share the same ⚠ + small-text visual cue. Cancellations are removed and replaced by a thin strip that names the affected preference but not the journey.

Decisions locked in v2 → v3: • Window: ±30 → −60 m / +15 m (skewed to early)
• Realtime delay + disruption remarks → same ⚠ block under leave-by
• Cancelled journey → thin strip: "B · journey cancelled" (no detail)
• API failure (v2 §12) — accepted as-is, not re-rendered here

1 · Asymmetric window — long lead, short tail

T sits 80% across (60 min early space on the left, 15 min late tail on the right). Late arrivals are still allowed but only just enough to keep "I might be 5 min late" visible; you don't see options that put you 30 min behind your deadline.

arrive-by 09:30 · window −60 / +15
on-time arrival (in window)
arrives late but inside +15 m tail
outside window — dropped

Same shape applies to all preferences; the lead-time / late-tail values are global defaults, with a per-preference override if needed (rarely).

2 · Auto-hide progression with the asymmetric window

Same idea as v2 §10, but the geometry is skewed. The preference now persists visibly long before T (full 60 min lead) and falls off quickly (~15 min) after T.

08:35 — early, full window

5 rows visible

09:25 — at T, last call

2 rows visible

09:46 — past T + 15, gone

preference auto-hides

3 · Unified ⚠ alert — delays and disruptions share the cue

A single visual treatment: a small ⚠ block under the leave-by HH:MM with concise text. Used for realtime delay (+4m), platform/lift advisories, line construction, anything BVG returns. Multiple alerts on one row stack vertically.

4 · Cancellation as a thin strip

A cancelled journey is removed entirely. In its place: a thin strip naming the affected preference ("B · journey cancelled") with a strikethrough — no pictogram, no times. The user knows something disappeared and which person it belonged to, but doesn't waste pixels on a journey that's gone.

5 · Multi-cancel and degenerate cases

What happens when several journeys for the same preference are cancelled in one window? The strips collapse into a single line: "B · 3 journeys cancelled". Avoids stacking three identical strikethrough lines.
